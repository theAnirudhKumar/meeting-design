# Decision Record

One record per decision. The format is a close cousin of the architecture decision record Michael Nygard described in 2011, applied to any decision a meeting makes. If a field cannot be filled from the notes, write what is missing in capitals instead of leaving it blank.

---

**Meeting:** [name, date]
**Decision:** [one sentence, in the past tense: "We will offer 15% on new annual deals."]
**Confidence:** [firm / needs confirming, and the hedge quoted if so]
**Decided by:** [name, or "group"]
**Rule used:** [Directive / Consult / Consent / Consensus / Delegate / RULE NOT STATED]
**Why:** [the reason given in the meeting, in one or two sentences, using only what the notes say]
**Effective:** [date, or NO DATE]
