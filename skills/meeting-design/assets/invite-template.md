# Invite Template

The paste-ready invite. Fill every bracket from what the user supplied, and leave a bracket empty rather than inventing a fact. Keep it under 150 words so people read it.

---

**Title:** [The decision, as a verb phrase. "Choose the Q4 launch date", not "Launch sync"]

**Length:** [minutes]  **Chair:** [name]

**Decision for this meeting:** By the end we will have decided [one sentence].

**How we decide:** [Directive / Consult / Consent / Consensus / Delegate, in one line, e.g. "Consult. I will hear everyone and decide by Friday."]

**If we do not decide:** [the default that takes effect / the named person and date it escalates to / the specific missing thing and who is fetching it]

**Read first:** [link or "nothing, we read it together in the first 10 minutes"]

**Agenda**
- [X min] [item, phrased as a question]
- [X min] [item, phrased as a question]
- [X min] **Decision:** [decider]
- [X min] Close: decision, owner, date, who gets told
- If we overrun, we cut: [named item]

**Who and why:** Decider: [name]. Contributors: [name, what we get wrong without them]. Informed, getting the notes: [names].
