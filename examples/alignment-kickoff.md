# Example: the 14-person alignment kickoff

*Illustrative. Invented project and people.* Skill: `meeting-design`, quick fix.

## The invite as pasted

> **Project Lantern: Alignment Kickoff**
> Thursday 14:00-15:30. 14 attendees.
> Let's get everyone aligned on the project and next steps. Agenda: intro, background, timeline, roles, open discussion.

## What the skill returned

**Verdict: Shrink, and split in two.** The invite mixes an Align meeting (everyone understands the plan) with a Decide meeting (timeline and roles) in one 90-minute slot. Fourteen people cannot decide anything; they can perform. The decisions go to the people who make them, and everyone else is aligned in writing and checked by saying it back.

**Cost:** 14 people x 1.5 hours x 60 = 1,260. *Assumption: 60 per hour per person.* After the change: 6 people x 45 minutes x 60 = 270 for the decision session, plus a written brief everyone replies to.

**Rewritten invite (the decision session)**

```
Title: Project Lantern: confirm the timeline and who owns each workstream
Length: 45 min   Chair: Priya

Decision for this meeting: By the end we will have decided the launch date and one owner for each of the four workstreams.

How we decide: Consult. Priya hears everyone and decides before the end of the meeting.

If we do not decide: Priya's draft timeline stands, and Priya assigns the unowned workstreams by Friday noon.

Read first: the one-page brief (link). We read it together in the first 10 minutes.

Agenda
- 10 min Silent read of the brief
- 10 min Questions of fact only
- 15 min Dates and owners: each person states their constraint, then Priya proposes
- 5 min  Decision
- 5 min  Close: dates, owners, who tells the wider team
- If we overrun, we cut: questions of fact (they continue in the brief's comments)

Who and why: Decider: Priya. Contributors: the four workstream leads (they will own the work) and the engineering manager (owns capacity).
Informed, getting the brief and the outcome: the other eight.
```

**For the other eight:** send the one-page brief and ask each person to reply with *"In my words, what this means for my team is..."* by Friday. This is the say-it-back check from the Align shape, done in writing.

**What I assumed:** that Priya is the decider; that the four workstream leads are the owners. Questions: is the launch date really open, or is it already fixed? If it is fixed, this is a Directive and the invite should say so.
