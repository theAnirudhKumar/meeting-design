# Example: the weekly sync with eleven people

*Illustrative. Invented team, invented people.* Skill: `meeting-design`, quick fix.

## The invite as pasted

> **Weekly Product Sync**
> Fridays 10:00-11:00, recurring. 11 attendees.
> Weekly sync to go over updates from each team and discuss blockers. Please come prepared to share.

## What the skill returned

**Verdict: Shrink.** Eleven people take turns reporting to one lead, so most of the hour is people listening to updates that do not affect them. Updates move to writing, and the call keeps only the part that needs people together: things that are stuck.

**Cost:** 11 people x 1 hour x 60 = 660 per meeting, about 2,860 a month at weekly. *Assumption: 60 per hour per person. Replace it with your own.* Time in the room only, so this is a floor.

**Rewritten invite**

```
Title: Product blockers: who is stuck and what unblocks them
Length: 25 min, Fridays   Chair: Maya

Decision for this meeting: By the end every blocked item has an owner and a next step.

How we decide: Delegate. The person who can unblock an item owns it; the group is input.

If we do not decide: The item goes to Maya, who assigns it by end of Monday.

Read first: the status thread. Add your update by Thursday 5pm: done, next, blocked.

Agenda
- 3 min  Read the blocked items from the thread
- 18 min Work each blocker with the people it involves. Others may leave
- 4 min  Close: owner and date per blocker
- If we overrun, we cut: any blocker that is not blocking a launch

Who and why: Decider: Maya. Contributors: leads of the teams with a blocker that week.
Informed, getting the thread: everyone else.
```

**Attendee cut:** Five stay: Maya (chair and decider), the engineering lead, the design lead, the data lead and the support lead, because those functions are where blockers usually land. The other six read the thread and join only when their item is named. *Assumption: those five functions produce most blockers. Check against the last month.*

**New cost:** 5 people x 25 minutes, about 9 person-hours a month against 47.7 before, so roughly 38.6 person-hours a month freed. The time to write updates is not counted either way, since people were preparing them already.

**What I assumed:** that Maya chairs; which functions are five; that no one needs the call for relationship reasons. Questions: who holds the final call on priorities, and is there a week where nobody has a blocker?
