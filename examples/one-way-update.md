# Example: the launch FYI call

*Illustrative. Invented team.* Skill: `meeting-design`, quick fix.

## The invite as pasted

> **Launch copy update**
> Wednesday 15:00-15:30. 6 attendees.
> Quick FYI on the final launch copy and where it will appear.

## What the skill returned

**Verdict: Cancel and replace with a doc.** One person has the information and five receive it. There is no decision named, and nothing in the description asks the room to disagree with anything.

**Cost:** 6 people x 0.5 hours x 60 = 180. *Assumption: 60 per hour per person.* The replacement costs the author about fifteen minutes and each reader a few.

**The replacement (a message, not an invite)**

```
Subject: Final launch copy, where it appears, and what to flag

The launch copy is final: [link]. It appears on [the product page, the launch email, the in-app banner].

Three things to check against your own area: [item], [item], [item].

If anything in it is wrong for your team, reply here by Wednesday noon and I will fix it before it ships. If I hear nothing, it ships as written. Anyone who wants a call, reply and I will set one up with whoever raised a concern.
```

**What I assumed:** that no one needs to approve the copy in the room. If someone does, this is a Decide meeting with a sign-off, and the quick fix should be rerun with that decision named.
