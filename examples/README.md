# Examples

Worked cases, each from one of the skills. Every company, person and number here is invented to be plausible. They are illustrations of the output, not records of real meetings, and not evidence of results.

The cost lines use a placeholder rate of 60 per hour per person, which is an assumption. Your own rate changes the money figures and nothing else.

| :--- | :--- | :--- |

| [The 14-person alignment kickoff](alignment-kickoff.md) | meeting-design | Shrink: a decision session of six, everyone else aligned in writing |
| [The customer quarterly review](customer-qbr.md) | meeting-design | Keep and fix: right size, wrong design |
| [The launch FYI call](one-way-update.md) | meeting-design | Cancel and replace with a doc |
