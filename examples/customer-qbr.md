# Example: the customer quarterly review

*Illustrative. Invented customer and people.* Skill: `meeting-design`, quick fix, customer-call playbook.

## The invite as pasted

> **Q3 Business Review: Ridgeway Supplies**
> Tuesday 11:00-12:00. 9 attendees: 4 from Ridgeway, 5 from our side.
> Review Q3 performance, product roadmap, and discuss Q4 plans.

## What the skill returned

**Verdict: Keep and fix.** The size is about right and the relationship needs the live time. The design is the problem: it is a presentation of our results with no ask, so the likeliest outcome is polite nodding and no change.

**Cost (our side only):** 5 people x 1 hour x 60 = 300. *Assumption: 60 per hour per person.* Ridgeway's side is theirs to count, and not a reason to trim it.

**Rewritten invite**

```
Title: Ridgeway Q4 priorities: agree the two outcomes we commit to
Length: 60 min   Chair: Jonas

Decision for this meeting: By the end we will have agreed the two outcomes that matter most to Ridgeway in Q4 and what each side does next.

How we decide: Consult toward Ridgeway. Elena tells us the priorities; we say what we can commit to and what we cannot.

If we do not decide: We send a written proposal of two outcomes within two working days and Elena replies by the following Friday.

Read first: the Q3 one-pager (link), sent Monday. We will not present it.

Agenda
- 5 min  Purpose and what we want to leave with. Elena can change it
- 10 min Ridgeway's priorities, in their words, before we speak
- 25 min Q3 results and roadmap, only as they bear on those priorities
- 15 min Q4: the two outcomes and what each side owns
- 5 min  Close: next steps, dates, and a written recap from us within a day
- If we overrun, we cut: roadmap items Ridgeway did not raise

Who and why: Ridgeway: Elena (sponsor, decides priorities), Sam and two others as they choose.
Ours: Jonas (chairs), Aisha (owns the account), Tomas (answers the hard product questions).
```

**Attendee cut (our side only):** From five to three. Aisha owns the account, Tomas can answer the product questions Ridgeway is likeliest to raise, and Jonas chairs. The other two get the written recap. Ridgeway's four are left alone.

**What I assumed:** that Elena is the sponsor; that Q3 numbers exist as a one-pager. Questions: is there an expansion or renewal conversation coming up that the Q4 outcomes should lead into? Is anyone on Ridgeway's side likely to be surprised by an item?
